## Section 1: Number Sense & Operations (Questions 1–2)

**1.** Simplify: 2³ × 2⁴
- A. 2⁷
- B. 2¹²
- C. 4⁷
- D. 8

**Answer: A (2⁷)** — When multiplying same base, add exponents: 3 + 4 = 7
*Standard: NC.8.EE.1*

---

**2.** Between which two integers does √50 lie?
- A. 6 and 7
- B. 7 and 8
- C. 8 and 9
- D. 24 and 26

**Answer: B (7 and 8)** — 7² = 49 and 8² = 64, so √50 is between 7 and 8
*Standard: NC.8.NS.2*

## Section 2: Expressions & Equations (Questions 3–6)

**3.** Solve for x: 2(x - 3) = 4x + 2
- A. x = -4
- B. x = -2
- C. x = 2
- D. x = 4

**Answer: A (x = -4)** — 2x - 6 = 4x + 2 → -2x = 8 → x = -4
*Standard: NC.8.EE.7*

---

**4.** What is the slope of the line passing through the points (2, 5) and (4, 11)?
- A. 2
- B. 3
- C. 6
- D. 8

**Answer: B (3)** — slope = (11 - 5) / (4 - 2) = 6 / 2 = 3
*Standard: NC.M1.F-IF.6*

---

**5.** Solve the system of equations: x + y = 10 and 2x - y = 5
- A. x = 5, y = 5
- B. x = 3, y = 7
- C. x = 7, y = 3
- D. x = 15, y = -5

**Answer: A (x = 5, y = 5)** — Add equations: 3x = 15, x = 5, then y = 5
*Standard: NC.M1.A-REI.6*

---

**6.** Factor the expression: x² - 5x + 6
- A. (x - 2)(x - 3)
- B. (x + 2)(x + 3)
- C. (x - 1)(x - 6)
- D. (x + 1)(x - 6)

**Answer: A ((x - 2)(x - 3))** — Two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to -5: -2 and -3
*Standard: NC.M1.A-SSE.3*

## Section 3: Functions (Questions 7–11)

**7.** Is the relation {(1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7), (1, 9)} a function?
- A. Yes
- B. No
- C. Only if graphed
- D. Cannot determine

**Answer: B (No)** — The input 1 maps to both 3 and 9, which violates the definition of a function
*Standard: NC.8.F.1*

---

**8.** If f(x) = 2x + 1, what is f(4)?
- A. 7
- B. 8
- C. 9
- D. 10

**Answer: C (9)** — f(4) = 2(4) + 1 = 8 + 1 = 9
*Standard: NC.M1.F-IF.2*

---

**9.** Which table represents a linear function?

| | x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. | y | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 |
| B. | y | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| C. | y | 1 | 4 | 9 | 16 |
| D. | y | 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 |

**Answer: B** — Constant rate of change (+2) indicates a linear function
*Standard: NC.8.F.3*

---

**10.** A ball is thrown upward. Its height in feet after t seconds is given by h(t) = -16t² + 48t + 4. At what time does the ball reach its maximum height?
- A. t = 1
- B. t = 1.5
- C. t = 2
- D. t = 3

**Answer: B (t = 1.5)** — Maximum at t = -b / 2a = -48 / (2 × -16) = 1.5
*Standard: NC.M1.F-IF.4*

---

**11.** Which of the following represents exponential growth?
- A. y = 3x + 2
- B. y = x²
- C. y = 2(1.5)ˣ
- D. y = 2(0.5)ˣ

**Answer: C (y = 2(1.5)ˣ)** — Base > 1 means growth; y = 2(0.5)ˣ is exponential decay
*Standard: NC.M1.F-LE.1*

## Section 4: Geometry (Questions 12–14)

**12.** A right triangle has legs of length 3 and 4. What is the length of the hypotenuse?
- A. 5
- B. 6
- C. 7
- D. 12

**Answer: A (5)** — 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25; √25 = 5
*Standard: NC.8.G.7*

---

**13.** What is the volume of a cylinder with radius 3 and height 10?
- A. 30π
- B. 60π
- C. 90π
- D. 900π

**Answer: C (90π)** — V = πr²h = π(9)(10) = 90π
*Standard: NC.8.G.9*

---

**14.** Point A is located at (1, 2) and Point B is located at (4, 6). What is the distance between them?
- A. 3
- B. 4
- C. 5
- D. 7

**Answer: C (5)** — d = √((4-1)² + (6-2)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5
*Standard: NC.8.G.8*

---

## Section 5: Statistics & Probability (Questions 15–16)

**15.** A bag contains 3 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. If you pick one marble without looking, what is the probability of picking a blue marble?
- A. 1/2
- B. 3/10
- C. 1/5
- D. 5/10

**Answer: A (1/2)** — 5 blue out of 10 total = 5/10 = 1/2. Note: D (5/10) is equivalent but not simplified.
*Standard: NC.7.SP.7*

---

**16.** A scatter plot shows a negative correlation. Which equation is most likely the line of best fit?
- A. y = 2x + 5
- B. y = -3x + 10
- C. y = x²
- D. y = 5

**Answer: B (y = -3x + 10)** — Negative correlation means the line has a negative slope
*Standard: NC.M1.S-ID.6*
