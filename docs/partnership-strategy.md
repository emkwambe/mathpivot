# MathPivot Partnership Alignment & Strategy

## Partnership Philosophy

MathPivot partners with professional organizations to:
1. Give students real career exposure (not abstract "math is useful" messaging)
2. Give parents confidence that their investment connects to professional outcomes
3. Give MathPivot institutional credibility that no marketing budget can buy
4. Give coaches access to professional networks and teaching resources

**Guardrail:** Every partner must have an existing youth program, a clean reputation, and a direct connection between math and a career pathway.

---

## Tier 1 Partners — Pursue Immediately

### 1. The Actuarial Foundation
- **What:** Philanthropic arm of the actuarial profession
- **Why #1:** Already runs Math Motivators (free tutoring grades 5-12), Modeling the Future Challenge ($60K scholarships), grade 6-8 curriculum ("Expect the Unexpected With Math"), and STEM Stars Scholars ($20K scholarships)
- **Alignment:** Structurally the closest match to MathPivot's model — they tutor, they compete, they connect to careers
- **Partnership model:** Co-brand career exposure modules, channel MathPivot students into Modeling the Future Challenge, use their curriculum in Foundation track
- **Contact:** actuarialfoundation.org
- **Career pathway shown:** Actuarial science, risk analysis, insurance, finance

### 2. MATHCOUNTS Foundation
- **What:** Premier middle school math competition organization (500+ events, all 50 states)
- **Why:** Gold standard for grades 6-8. Direct demographic overlap with MathPivot Foundation track. Free for Title I schools.
- **Alignment:** Mathathlon is MathPivot's accessible on-ramp; MATHCOUNTS is the next step for top performers
- **Partnership model:** MathPivot as official MATHCOUNTS prep platform, co-host local practice sessions, share competition calendar
- **Contact:** mathcounts.org
- **Career pathway shown:** Math excellence as a gateway to all STEM careers

### 3. MAA (Mathematical Association of America)
- **What:** Operates AMC 8/10/12 → AIME → USAMO → IMO pipeline
- **Why:** AMC is the most recognized math competition in the US. AMC prep is a natural use case for Acceleration and Elite tracks.
- **Alignment:** MathPivot prepares → students compete in AMC → top performers reach AIME/USAMO
- **Partnership model:** Official AMC prep resource listing, competition calendar integration, coach training on AMC problem types
- **Contact:** maa.org
- **Career pathway shown:** Mathematical sciences, research, academia

### 4. NSBE Pre-College Initiative
- **What:** National Society of Black Engineers — 170+ NSBE Jr. chapters, 5,000+ K-12 students
- **Why:** Try-Math-A-Lon is a math tutoring/competition program for underrepresented students. Natural co-delivery partner.
- **Alignment:** MathPivot's coaching model can plug into NSBE Jr. chapters as the math prep engine
- **Partnership model:** Provide MathPivot access to NSBE Jr. chapters, co-host Mathathlon events at NSBE conferences, scholarship fund for NSBE members
- **Contact:** nsbe.org
- **Career pathway shown:** Engineering (all disciplines)

### 5. SIAM (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics)
- **What:** Professional society for applied math — runs M3 Challenge ($150K+ in prizes)
- **Why:** M3 Challenge is a math modeling competition that directly connects school math to real careers
- **Alignment:** Elite track students prepare for M3 Challenge through applied math projects
- **Partnership model:** MathPivot as M3 Challenge prep platform, host SIAM Visiting Lecturers for career exposure sessions
- **Contact:** siam.org
- **Career pathway shown:** Applied math in industry, engineering, government, tech

## Tier 2 Partners — Pursue in Year 1

### Statistics & Data Science
**American Statistical Association (ASA)**
- "This Is Statistics" campaign shows students why math matters in data careers
- Free student membership for high schoolers
- Co-brand career modules: "What does a data scientist do?"
- **Career pathway:** Data science, analytics, research

### Economics & Finance
**Council for Economic Education (CEE)**
- National Economics Challenge (11K students) and Personal Finance Challenge (18K students)
- Free K-12 curriculum (EconEdLink)
- MathPivot students prep for economics competitions
- **Career pathway:** Economics, finance, policy

**Junior Achievement (JA)**
- Free K-12 financial literacy programs, volunteer-delivered
- JA Finance Park capstone simulation
- MathPivot coaches could serve as JA volunteers
- **Career pathway:** Entrepreneurship, business, personal finance

### Computer Science & AI
**AI4ALL**
- Summer programs at Stanford/Princeton requiring Algebra 2+
- MathPivot as the "math readiness" pipeline for AI4ALL
- Shared focus on underrepresented students
- **Career pathway:** AI, machine learning, data engineering

### Engineering
**Society of Women Engineers (SWENext)**
- Free membership ages 5-18, 200+ outreach events/year
- SWENext Clubs in schools, Leadership Academy
- Co-host events connecting math to engineering careers
- **Career pathway:** All engineering disciplines

**IEEE (TryEngineering)**
- Career exploration ages 8-18, IEEE Spark publication ages 14-18
- STEM grants ($70K/year) could fund joint programming
- Massive professional member base for mentors/speakers
- **Career pathway:** Electrical engineering, computer science, robotics

### Healthcare
**HOSA - Future Health Professionals**
- 220K members, 82 competitive events requiring quantitative skills
- MathPivot strengthens the math needed for health science competitions
- **Career pathway:** Healthcare, biostatistics, epidemiology, nursing

## Tier 3 Partners — Institutional Credibility

### Math Education Authority
**National Council of Teachers of Mathematics (NCTM)**
- Endorsement from NCTM = maximum credibility with schools, districts, teachers
- 90,000 members, 250 affiliates nationwide
- Curriculum validation for MathPivot's atomic concept approach
- **Value:** Institutional trust, not career pathway

### STEM Pipeline (Diversity)
**Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE)**
- SHPE Jr. chapters at middle/high schools
- Hispanic STEM pipeline — underserved market
- **Career pathway:** Engineering, technology

**National Action Council for Minorities in Engineering (NACME)**
- Closing math gaps in underserved communities — MathPivot's exact value proposition
- Summer engineering programs, high school academies
- Corporate partnerships (major engineering firms) extend career exposure
- **Career pathway:** Engineering careers for underrepresented students

**ASPPH (Schools of Public Health)**
- Student Journey initiative, Summer Institute in Biostatistics
- Shows how algebra/statistics connect to disease tracking, clinical trials
- **Career pathway:** Epidemiology, biostatistics, public health

## Implementation Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)
1. **Reach out to Actuarial Foundation** — request curriculum partnership for grade 6-8 content
2. **Register for MATHCOUNTS** — list MathPivot students in local competitions
3. **Add AMC dates to competition calendar** — no partnership needed, just integrate
4. **Build career module content** linking each partner's career to specific math concepts

### Phase 2: Formalize (Months 4-6)
5. **Apply for SIAM Visiting Lecture Program** — get industry speakers for virtual sessions
6. **Contact NSBE Pre-College Initiative** — propose pilot in 3 NSBE Jr. chapters
7. **Join ASA "This Is Statistics"** — co-create data science career module
8. **Apply for IEEE STEM grant** — fund joint programming

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12)
9. **Seek NCTM endorsement** — curriculum validation
10. **Partner with AI4ALL** — position as math readiness pipeline
11. **Co-host regional Mathathlon** with NSBE/SHPE chapters
12. **Launch scholarship fund** with Actuarial Foundation STEM Stars alignment

## Partnership Guardrails

1. **No pay-to-play.** MathPivot does not pay for endorsements. Value exchange only.
2. **Student safety first.** Every partner must have existing youth-facing programs with appropriate safeguards.
3. **No exclusivity.** MathPivot partners across verticals — no single org owns our career exposure pipeline.
4. **Curriculum independence.** Partners inform content but don't control the atomic curriculum.
5. **Transparency with parents.** All partnerships disclosed. No hidden commercial arrangements.
6. **Reputation check.** Any partner must pass a basic reputation review before logo goes on the site.
