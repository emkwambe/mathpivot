# MathPivot Strategic Analysis

## Market Position
MathPivot at $349-799/mo sits between Mathnasium ($200-400) and premium 1:1 tutoring ($1,000+/mo equivalent). Elite at $799/mo is 35-40% cheaper than equivalent 1:1 hours but delivers a structured coaching experience no 1:1 tutor provides.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Model | Monthly Cost | Group Size | Named Coach | Mastery Tracking |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-----------|------------|-----------------|
| Kumon | Worksheet franchise | $160-200/subject | 100+/center | No | No |
| Mathnasium | Learning center | $200-400 | 50-100/center | No | Basic |
| Russian School of Math | Company-owned | $225-440 | 20+/class | No | No |
| AoPS Academy | Expert instructors | $675-1,125/course | 15-20/class | No | No |
| Private 1:1 | Random tutor | $800-1,280/mo equiv | 1 | Random | No |
| **MathPivot** | **Named coach coaching** | **$349-799** | **5-6/cohort** | **Yes** | **Concept-level** |

## Closest Comparable: Russian School of Mathematics
- Company-owned (no franchising), quality-gated expansion
- 85+ locations, ~$95.4M revenue, ~80K students
- ~$1.1M/location average
- Expands only when qualified teachers are available
- MathPivot's coach-led approach is the closest analog

## Unit Economics

### Direct Coaching (Current)
- Foundation: $349/mo × 5 students = $1,745/cohort. Coach 60% = $1,047. MathPivot 40% = $698.
- Elite: $799/mo × 5 students = $3,995/cohort. Coach 60% = $2,397. MathPivot 40% = $1,598.
- Coach with 3 cohorts: $3,141-7,191/mo income. MathPivot: $2,094-4,794/mo per coach.

### Licensed Coach (Future)
- Platform fee: $199/mo + 15% royalty
- Coach charges $349/mo × 15 students = $5,235/mo
- MathPivot receives: $199 + $785 = $984/mo per coach
- At 50 licensed coaches: $49,200/mo = $590K/year (near-zero marginal cost)

### Summer Clinics (Customer Acquisition)
- $249-449 per student, 100% credited toward year-round enrollment
- Effective CAC: $0 if they convert (clinic fee becomes first month credit)
- Payback period: < 1 month

## 3-5 Year Roadmap

### Year 1: Prove the Model ($150K-250K)
- Summer clinics in NC (4 programs, 2-3 coaches)
- 30-40% summer → year-round conversion
- 50-80 active year-round students
- Document MathPivot Method as teachable system

### Year 2: First Licensed Coaches ($400K-600K)
- Launch certification ($1,500-2,500)
- 10-15 certified coaches in 3-5 states
- Platform fee ($199/mo) + royalty (15%)
- School partnership pilot (2-3 schools)

### Year 3: Platform Revenue > Direct ($800K-1.2M)
- 30-50 licensed coaches nationally
- Assessment-only product for schools ($10-25/student)
- Master Coach tier enables training-of-trainers
- Summer clinic kits for licensed coaches

### Year 4-5: National Scale ($2M-5M)
- 100+ licensed coaches
- School district partnerships
- MathPivot certification = industry credential
- Potential acquisition target at 5-10x revenue

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Licensed coaches deliver poor quality | Certification + calibration + parent pulse monitoring |
| Competitors copy the model | Speed to market + teacher network effects |
| Pricing pressure | Diagnostic-driven outcomes justify premium |
| Founder dependency | MathPivot Method documentation + Master Coach tier |
| Coach churn | 85% revenue share for licensed coaches is competitive |

## The Bottom Line
MathPivot's product is not coaching hours. It's the MathPivot Method — the curriculum, assessments, training, certification, and operating system that enables thousands of students to receive exceptional math coaching through a network of capable coaches.
