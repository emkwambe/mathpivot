# MathPivot Marketing & Go-To-Market Strategy

MathPivot's product IS its marketing. The free diagnostic is the lead magnet. The summer clinic is the conversion event. The referral program is the growth engine.

## The MathPivot Marketing Funnel

```
FREE DIAGNOSTIC (/diagnostic)     ← Zero cost, captures lead + assessment data
        ↓
SUMMER CLINIC ($249-449)          ← Low-risk entry, 100% fee credit
        ↓
YEAR-ROUND COACHING ($349-799/mo) ← Recurring revenue, named coach
        ↓
REFERRAL ($50 credit)             ← Satisfied parent refers 1-2 families
        ↓
REPEAT
```

## Partnership Targets

### Free (Zero Cost)
- PTA/PTO partnerships (diagnostic assessment nights)
- Homeschool co-ops (free workshops, compliance exports)
- MATHCOUNTS/AMC (competition prep alignment)
- Libraries (workshop space, community boards)
- Churches/community centers (venue + trust)
- Boys & Girls Clubs, YMCA

### Low Cost ($200-500)
- Travel sports league sponsorship
- Pediatrician waiting room flyers
- Real estate agent cross-referral
- College prep company partnerships

### Revenue Share ($0 upfront)
- Outschool workshops (30% commission, top-of-funnel)
- Wyzant discovery sessions (25% commission)
