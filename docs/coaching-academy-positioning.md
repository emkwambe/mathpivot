# MathPivot Coaching Academy

## Product, Pricing, Positioning, and Program Design

### Purpose

This document establishes the business philosophy, coaching model, pricing rationale, marketing language, and platform direction for MathPivot.

All product decisions, website content, marketing materials, enrollment workflows, and future features should align with this vision.

---

## Executive Summary

MathPivot is not a tutoring company.

MathPivot is a mathematics coaching academy.

We help students build confidence, achieve mastery, and expand future opportunities through structured coaching programs, personalized learning roadmaps, and micro-cohort learning environments.

Parents are not purchasing tutoring hours.

They are investing in a long-term development pathway.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Most students do not struggle because they lack ability.

They struggle because learning gaps accumulate over time.

Traditional tutoring often focuses on immediate problems:

* Homework help
* Test preparation
* Last-minute intervention

While useful, this approach is reactive and often fails to address the underlying causes of long-term mathematical difficulty.

MathPivot focuses on systematic development.

We identify learning gaps, build a personalized roadmap, track mastery, and help students develop the confidence and thinking skills required for long-term success.

## Coaching Model

### Micro-Cohort Coaching

MathPivot operates using small coaching cohorts.

#### Capacity Rules

Target Cohort Size: 5 students

Maximum Cohort Size: 6 students

New cohorts should be created before routinely exceeding the target size.

#### Why This Matters

Students learn best when they can:

* Ask questions freely
* Participate actively
* Receive individual feedback
* Learn from peers
* Build accountability

Micro-cohorts provide:

* More personalization than large learning centers
* More collaboration than one-on-one tutoring
* Greater affordability than private coaching
* Stronger engagement and accountability

This cohort design is a strategic differentiator and should be highlighted throughout marketing and enrollment materials.

## Pricing Philosophy

MathPivot does not sell tutoring hours.

We sell development pathways.

Parents should not be encouraged to compare:

* Cost per hour
* Session duration
* Number of hours purchased

Instead, communication should focus on:

* Outcomes
* Progress
* Mastery
* Confidence
* Development
* Opportunities

The value of MathPivot comes from the complete coaching system rather than individual sessions.

## Enrollment Philosophy

The enrollment experience should feel like joining an academy, not purchasing tutoring hours.

Recommended flow:

1. Parent Inquiry
2. Diagnostic Assessment
3. Readiness Evaluation
4. Pathway Recommendation
5. Enrollment

The diagnostic assessment should be central to the enrollment experience.
