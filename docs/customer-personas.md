## Persona 1: The Proactive Suburban Parent
**"My child isn't failing — but they're not excelling either."**

- **Demographics:** HHI $100K+, suburban, college-educated, ages 35-50
- **Children:** Grades 5-8, at or slightly above grade level
- **Trigger:** School transition, sees peers in RSM/Kumon, wants competitive edge
- **Current spend:** $1,300-3,000/year on supplementary education
- **Pain points:** Rotating tutors, no mastery tracking, can't see real progress
- **Values:** Outcomes over hours, structured programs, visible progress
- **Where they are:** PTA meetings, Facebook parent groups, travel sports sidelines

**Why MathPivot fits:** Named coach who stays. Mastery dashboard. Structured programs. Career exposure makes math relevant.

**Acquisition:** Referral, free diagnostic event at school, Facebook ads

---

## Persona 2: The Travel Ball Family
**"We invest in development, not participation trophies."**

- **Demographics:** HHI $120K+, suburban, dual-income, ages 35-50
- **Children:** Grades 6-10, athletically competitive
- **Trigger:** Realizes academic scholarships > athletic scholarships
- **Current spend:** $10,000-20,000/year on travel sports
- **Pain points:** Heavy sports spend with uncertain ROI, math confidence lagging
- **Values:** Named coaches, structured seasons, competition, measurable development

**Why MathPivot fits:** "The travel ball of math" — same model they already believe in. $4-10K/year is 30-50% of what they spend on sports, for higher ROI.

**Acquisition:** Sports league cross-promo, travel ball practice flyers, coach referrals

## Persona 3: The Homeschool Family
**"I can teach reading. Math past pre-algebra terrifies me."**

- **Demographics:** 3.4M homeschool students, growing 4.9%/year
- **Children:** Grades 6-12, math is #1 outsourced subject
- **Trigger:** Pre-algebra (grade 6-7), parent can no longer teach effectively
- **Current spend:** $1,295-1,636/year total, $200-800 on math curriculum
- **Pain points:** Sequential math requires expertise, no accountability, compliance docs

**Why MathPivot fits:** Expert coaching for the one subject they can't do. Mastery tracking IS curriculum documentation. Compliance exports built in. Peer interaction in small groups.

**Acquisition:** Homeschool co-op presentations, homeschool Facebook groups, church networks

## Persona 4: The "My Child is Falling Behind" Parent
**"Their grades dropped and I don't know why."**

- **Demographics:** HHI $75K+, any geography, ages 30-50
- **Children:** Grades 6-9, recent grade drop, disengaged
- **Trigger:** Report card shock, teacher conference, "I hate math"
- **Current spend:** $0-500/year (urgency is new)
- **Pain points:** Doesn't know where the gap is, homework help didn't work

**Why MathPivot fits:** Free diagnostic tells them exactly where the gap is in 15 minutes. Summer clinic ($249) is low-risk entry. Named coach rebuilds confidence. Weekly mastery updates show improvement.

**Acquisition:** Google Ads, free diagnostic page, school counselor referral

---

## Persona 5: The Competition Math Family
**"My child doesn't need help. They need a challenge."**

- **Demographics:** HHI $100K+, urban/suburban, often highly educated families
- **Children:** Grades 6-10, above grade level, interested in AMC/MATHCOUNTS
- **Trigger:** Bored in school, wants competition prep. 300K+ register for AMC annually.
- **Current spend:** $2,000-5,000/year (AoPS $675-1,125/course, RSM $225-440/mo)
- **Pain points:** AoPS is self-paced (no coach), RSM has 20+ student classes

**Why MathPivot fits:** Mathathlon + AMC/MATHCOUNTS prep. Small groups (5-6) with named coach. Career exposure connects talent to futures. Elite at $799/mo competitive with RSM + AoPS combined.

**Acquisition:** MATHCOUNTS/AMC partnership, math competition groups, gifted education networks

---

## Persona 6: The School Partner
**"We need structured after-school math support."**

- **Demographics:** Title I schools, charter networks, district administrators
- **Trigger:** ESSER expired (tutoring dropped 49% → 43%), 73% below proficient
- **Pain points:** <2% get quality tutoring, generic programs show no results

**Why MathPivot fits:** Diagnostic-driven placement, mastery tracking for admin data, certified coaches (not substitutes), small groups within research-optimal range, state-aligned assessments.

**Acquisition:** District conferences, school board presentations, Title I outreach
